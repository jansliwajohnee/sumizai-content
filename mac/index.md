## From question to knowledge in three steps

1. **Ask.** Chat with the model you prefer: Claude, GPT, Gemini, Llama via Groq, hundreds via OpenRouter, or a model running locally in Ollama.
2. **It becomes a note.** When the answer is done, SumizAI writes one of your AI notes: a clear title, a one-line summary, the key points. The original question and answer stay word for word.
3. **It files itself.** The note lands in the right chapter of your table of contents, links to the notes it relates to, and merges with a near-duplicate instead of piling up.

## Everything your AI notes need. Nothing you have to maintain.

- **A table of contents that writes itself.** Chapters are laid out from what you actually ask about, and every new note is filed where it belongs.
- **No near-duplicates.** Ask the same thing twice in March and May and you get one richer note, not two half-notes.
- **Links that never break.** Notes link to each other and show what links back. Rename a note and every link follows.
- **Search that remembers the conversation.** Find the sentence you half-remember. Search reads titles, summaries, notes and the original chat.
- **Bring what you already have.** Paste text, drop Markdown and text files or a PDF — each one becomes notes in your library.

- **Notes in your language.** Ask in English, keep notes in Arabic, Czech or Japanese. Source material is translated, the original kept.
- **Vaults that keep topics apart.** Work, research and a side project each get their own library, language and table of contents.

## Private by design

Your notes are plain Markdown files in a folder on your Mac. Open them in any editor, back them up with Time Machine, keep them forever. They work without SumizAI.

- **Your notes never reach our servers.** Vaults live only on your Mac. We cannot read them, because we never receive them.
- **Your key, your provider.** The app talks straight from your Mac to the AI provider you chose, with your own API key — no middleman, no markup on tokens.
- **Fully offline AI if you want it.** Connect a local Ollama and not a single word leaves your computer.
- **No ads. No selling data..** Your account exists only to hold your subscription and to let you use it on your Macs. Delete it in the app at any time.

## Questions

### Do I need an AI API key?

Yes. SumizAI works with your own key from Anthropic, OpenAI, Google Gemini, Groq or OpenRouter, a server of your own, or a free local model in Ollama. You pay your provider directly at their rates, which is why the subscription can stay this small.

### Where are my notes stored?

In a folder on your Mac, as one Markdown file per note. Nothing is uploaded to us. You can open, copy, sync or back up the files like any others.

### What happens if I cancel?

Your AI notes stay exactly where they are, as plain files you own. The subscription pays for the app's AI features; your library never depends on it.

### Which Macs are supported?

Any Mac with macOS 12 Monterey or newer, Apple silicon or Intel.

### Which languages does it speak?

The app is available in 16 languages, and each vault can keep its notes in a language of its own.
