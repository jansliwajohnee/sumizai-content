# Goodnotes and SumizAI: a pen, or a conversation

Goodnotes is digital paper. You write with a stylus on a tablet, rest your palm, scribble to erase, mark up PDFs and search your own handwriting later. SumizAI has no pen and no canvas: it is a Mac app where you talk to an AI model and the replies worth keeping turn into typed AI notes, filed into chapters by the model.

The two barely overlap, which makes the choice simpler than on most comparison pages. Goodnotes facts and prices were read on goodnotes.com on 23 October 2026.

A typed note made from a chat. There is no handwriting layer in this app.

## What each app does, in eleven rows

|  | SumizAI | GoodNotes |
|---|---|---|
| Input | A chat with an AI model | Handwriting, typing, PDFs, audio |
| Who writes | The model writes and files; you edit | You write; AI summarises and searches |
| Price | 7 days free, then 1 USD a month | Free (3 files); Essential 11.99 USD a year; Pro 35.99 USD a year; one-time Special Edition on Apple |
| AI | Seven providers on your own API key | Goodnotes AI by plan; AI Pass add-on 9.99 USD a month |
| Devices | Mac first, via the Mac App Store soon; Windows, iOS, Android in preparation | iPad and iPhone, Mac, Windows, Android, web |
| Handwriting | None | Yes, searchable, convertible to text |
| PDF markup | No; PDFs are imported as text | Yes, highlights and pen on any PDF |
| Organisation | Table of contents, 6 levels, chosen by the model | Folders and notebooks |
| Sharing | Single user, no sync | Real-time collaboration; cloud library sync on Pro |
| File format | Plain `.md` | Goodnotes documents |
| Export | Vault ZIP, PDF in two levels, PowerPoint with 15 themes | Goodnotes document, PDF (editable or flattened), images |

## Goodnotes wins for anyone who writes by hand

If you own an iPad and a stylus, Goodnotes is hard to beat, and this app does not try. Concretely:

- handwritten lecture or meeting notes, searchable later;
- signing, highlighting and commenting on contracts and reports;
- sketches, diagrams and an infinite whiteboard for working with others in real time;
- audio recording and transcription with a summary (Essential and up; 20 minutes on Free);
- Windows, Android and a web version available today.

The Free plan holds only 3 files in total, so most regular users end up on Essential at 11.99 USD a year, which is cheap for what it does.

## This app wins when the knowledge comes from AI

A different person reads this page too: someone who types questions into Claude or GPT all day and never picks up a pen. For them, digital paper solves nothing. The useful material is already text, it just sits in a chat that scrolls away.

Here every reply you keep becomes a note with a title, a one-line summary, an expanded body and the original question and answer stored underneath, unchanged. The model files it into a numbered chapter, links it with Markdown links only to notes that exist, and checks for a near-duplicate first, with a second model scoring similarity from 0 to 1. A merge only appends.

The next question benefits from the earlier ones: it goes out with the table of contents and up to five relevant AI notes, 24,000 characters at most, and the app shows which notes it read. Goodnotes AI works on what you wrote; this works on what the model said.

If what you really want is an Obsidian alternative that fills itself from chats, rather than a Goodnotes replacement, see the [Obsidian alternative comparison](https://sumizai.com/alternative-to/sumizai-alternative-to-obsidian.html), written for exactly that reader. Since every note is a plain `.md` file, the exported vault opens in Obsidian as it is. That makes it an alternative to Obsidian for the collecting part, not for drawing.

## A kitchen renovation, two notebooks

You are redoing a small kitchen. In Goodnotes you sketch the floor plan with the pencil, mark the window and the gas pipe, import the supplier's PDF quote and circle the line with the wrong worktop length. Then you write “ask about 60 cm vs 65 cm depth” in the margin. All of that is visual and physical, and it suits the job.

In SumizAI you ask the model four things over two evenings: which tile adhesive class a wall behind the hob needs, how far a hood should sit above gas burners, whether a laminate worktop survives next to a sink, and what a 65 cm worktop changes for standard cabinets. Each kept answer becomes one of your AI notes under a chapter called “Kitchen”, with subchapters the model proposes, such as `1.1` “Walls and tiles”.

You would want both here, honestly. The sketch lives in Goodnotes. The reasoning, with its sources, lives in the vault, and a PDF export with summaries only gives the fitter a two-page brief.

## Getting notes from one app into the other

**Goodnotes to this app.** Goodnotes exports documents as Goodnotes files, as PDF (editable or flattened) or as images, and a whole folder as a ZIP. Choose PDF. The importer here accepts `.pdf` along with `.md`, `.markdown` and `.txt`, and works on the text it can read in each file, one file at a time. Typed documents and annotated PDFs carry text; a page that is only drawings gives the model little to work with. Each file becomes one of your AI notes with its text kept as the source.

**This app to Goodnotes.** Export the vault as a PDF, either with summaries only or with full notes, and open it in Goodnotes. Then you can annotate your AI notes with the pen like any other PDF. That works well for printing a chapter to review on the iPad.

## Goodnotes and AI notes, briefly

### Does SumizAI support handwriting or Apple Pencil?

No. It is a typed, chat-based app for the Mac. For pen input use Goodnotes.

### How much is Goodnotes?

There is a Free plan limited to 3 files. Essential costs 11.99 USD a year, Pro 35.99 USD a year, and Apple users can buy a one-time Special Edition; the page does not show its price.

### Does Goodnotes have AI?

Yes. Summaries, search across typed text, handwriting and sketches, math help and, on Pro, Create Mode and Meetings AI. Usage is limited by credits, with an AI Pass for more.

### Can I read my AI notes in Goodnotes?

Yes, as a PDF. Export the vault or a chapter as PDF and open it there to mark it up.
