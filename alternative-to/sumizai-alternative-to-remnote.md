# SumizAI vs RemNote: remember it, or keep it?

RemNote is a study tool. You write notes, mark parts of them as flashcards, and the app brings each card back at the moment you are about to forget it; PDFs, slides and an AI quiz generator sit around that core. SumizAI has no flashcards at all. It takes answers from your conversations with a model and keeps them as AI notes in chapters, so the question is whether you need to memorise material or to collect it.

Students preparing for an exam and people building a reference library from chats want different things, and this page is blunt about which is which. RemNote facts and prices: remnote.com, read on 23 October 2026.

Notes in a vault, each with a one-line summary. No cards and no review queue.

## Where the chat-first approach wins

If you are not sitting an exam, the review queue is overhead. Plenty of people use a model like a colleague: how do I price a freelance project, what does this lease clause mean, why is my sourdough flat. They do not need to recite the answer in a month. They need to find it again.

That is the job here. The model writes a title, a one-line summary and an expanded body, and keeps your question and its full reply underneath as the source. It picks a chapter by number, say `4.2` “Contracts”, or asks to open a new subchapter. Links go only to notes that exist, since the model is handed a map of titles and file names, and each note lists its backlinks.

Pricing is flatter too. RemNote meters AI in credits per plan. This app charges 1 USD a month and you pay the provider for tokens: Anthropic, OpenAI, Google Gemini, Groq, OpenRouter, a local Ollama or any OpenAI-compatible server.

## Exam week, both ways

Take a second-year student revising photosynthesis for Friday.

In RemNote she writes “Calvin cycle :: fixes CO2 into sugar using ATP and NADPH” and that line is already a card. She uploads the lecture slides, highlights the diagram, and asks the AI to make ten practice questions. On Thursday the queue serves her the four cards she keeps missing. This is what RemNote is for, and it does it well.

With AI notes, she asks the model to explain the light reactions, then the Calvin cycle, then why C4 plants cope better with heat. Three AI notes land in the chapter “Plant biology”, each with a summary. On Thursday she exports the chapter as a PDF with summaries only, two pages to read on the bus. Useful, but nothing will quiz her.

For the exam itself, RemNote. For the term paper she writes next month, where she needs to cite what she asked and what she was told, the vault of AI notes with untouched sources is the better archive.

## Choose RemNote when

- you have to memorise: vocabulary, anatomy, case law, formulas;
- you study from PDFs and slides and want to annotate them next to your notes;
- you want lecture recording and transcription (on Pro with AI);
- you need it on Windows, Linux, iPhone or Android today, synced across devices;
- you want to move cards into Anki later, which RemNote exports as an `.apkg` file.

None of that exists here, and it is not planned as far as this page knows. The same goes for anyone hunting for Obsidian alternatives with built-in review: RemNote has it, this app does not.

## Carrying notes between the two

**RemNote out.** Under Settings, Export, RemNote offers its complete format, OPML, Anki (flashcards only), HTML, Markdown and indented text. For this app pick Markdown. Then use the import panel: choose files or a folder, and the importer reads `.md`, `.markdown`, `.txt` and `.pdf` one file at a time, turning each into one of your AI notes with the original text kept as its source. Flashcard syntax stays as plain text in that source; nothing schedules reviews afterwards.

**Vault out.** The ZIP holds `base.md` plus a `notes/` folder of Markdown files with relative links. I checked RemNote's export options for this page, not its import options, so try one file before moving a whole vault.

A small aside for readers comparing several tools: if you also use Obsidian, the [Obsidian alternative that writes from your chats](https://sumizai.com/alternative-to/sumizai-alternative-to-obsidian.html) page explains how the same `.md` export opens directly in Obsidian, where your own plugins (spaced-repetition ones included) can take over. Used like that, the app is an alternative to Obsidian for collecting, and Obsidian stays the place for studying.

## RemNote vs AI notes: quick questions

### Can this app make flashcards?

No. It writes and files notes; there is no card format and no review schedule.

### How much does RemNote cost?

Free covers unlimited notes and flashcards with limits such as 3 annotated PDFs. Pro is 8 USD a month or 96 USD a year, Pro with AI 18 USD a month or 216 USD a year.

### Does RemNote work offline?

Yes, according to remnote.com you can take notes offline and sync when you reconnect.

### Which keeps the original AI answer?

This app does by design: the question and reply sit under each note word for word, and merges only append. RemNote stores what you or its AI write into your notes.

### Can I export from RemNote to Markdown?

Yes. Markdown is one of six export formats listed in RemNote's help centre.
