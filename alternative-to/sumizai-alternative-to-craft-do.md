# SumizAI vs Craft: writing a document or keeping the answers

Craft is a document editor for Mac, iPad, iPhone, Windows and the web, known for native apps and careful design: blocks, nested pages, whiteboards, collections and one-click publishing. SumizAI is narrower. It saves what an AI model tells you as AI notes, each with a summary, a source and a chapter, without you opening an editor.

If you produce documents, Craft is the better tool and this page says so first. If you collect answers, read on. Prices and limits were taken from craft.do and its help centre on 23 October 2026.

## Craft versus the vault, row by row

## Stay with Craft if you write for readers

A client proposal. A trip plan with a map on a whiteboard. A weekly newsletter draft that three family members comment on. That is Craft's ground, and it is good at it.

- Documents look finished. Craft has won an Apple Design Award, and the native apps feel it.
- Whiteboards, a calendar view, daily notes and tasks inside documents.
- Collections act as small databases for tracking things.
- Publishing a page to the web, or sharing a Space on the Family plan.
- Claude, ChatGPT, Cursor or Raycast can read and write your documents over MCP.

None of that exists in an app for AI notes. It has no editor for long writing and no sharing at all.

## A document versus a stack of answers

Take a Friday afternoon spent on a kitchen renovation. In Craft you would open a page called “Kitchen”, paste quotes from three builders, sketch the layout on a whiteboard and share it with your partner. You are the author, and the document is the result.

Now the evening. You ask GPT how much a load-bearing wall changes the cost, then Claude whether induction needs a separate circuit, then a third question about tiles. These are not documents. Each answer becomes one of your AI notes: titled by the model, summarised in one line, filed under “3. Kitchen” with sub-chapters for electrics and walls, with the full reply kept as its source.

On Sunday you ask, “What did I learn about the wiring?” The question goes out with the table of contents and the five most relevant notes at most, 24,000 characters in total, and the screen tells you which notes the model read. Craft's MCP link works the other way round: an outside assistant reaches into documents you wrote.

The notes list: every entry was written from a chat, with its summary under the title.

## When the vault app is the better fit

Choose it if most of your useful text comes from chats with an AI and you never get round to rewriting it into documents. Choose it if you want the model of your choice, from seven providers on your own key, including a local Ollama model, instead of a credit count. And choose it if you want your notes as files on your own disk with no cloud between you and them.

Price is part of it too. One plan at 1 USD a month, with your model usage billed by the provider. Craft's free plan stops at 1,500 blocks, so heavy users end up on Plus.

The [AI notes app](https://sumizai.com/) page shows the whole path from question to filed note.

## SumizAI as an Obsidian alternative, for Craft users who want files

Craft users who leave for plain files tend to land on Obsidian. If that is your plan, read [how this Obsidian alternative works with Obsidian](https://sumizai.com/alternative-to/sumizai-alternative-to-obsidian.html): the exported vault is a folder of `.md` files with `base.md` as the table of contents, and Obsidian opens it directly.

## Moving documents from Craft

Craft exports single documents as PDF, Word, image, Markdown or TextBundle; bulk export of several documents is available on Mac and iPad (Markdown, TextBundle, PDF), and whole folders export only from the Mac app. Pick Markdown.

The importer here reads `.md`, `.markdown`, `.txt` and `.pdf`, single files or a folder, one file at a time. Each document becomes an AI note with the exported text attached as its source, and the table of contents is rebuilt after every file. Whiteboards and collections do not come across. Import a test folder of ten documents before the rest: every file is a model call on your own key.

## Craft or AI notes from chats? Questions and answers

### Is Craft free?

There is a free plan with 1,500 blocks, 1 GB of storage and 15 AI credits. Plus removes the content limits and gives 50 AI credits a month.

### Which one has better AI?

They use it differently. Craft's AI helps you write inside a document and connects to outside assistants over MCP. Here the AI writes, files and links the notes, and you bring the model.

### Can a note from the vault go on the web, as in Craft?

No. You can export a PDF or an editable PowerPoint deck and send that.

### Does it work on iPhone like Craft?

Not yet. The Mac version comes first; iOS, Windows and Android are in preparation.
