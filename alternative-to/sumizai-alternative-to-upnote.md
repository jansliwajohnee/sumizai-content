# UpNote or AI notes for a semester of questions

UpNote is a good-looking notebook for Mac, Windows, Linux, iOS and Android: nested notebooks, hashtags, Markdown, sync in the background. SumizAI sits on a different shelf. It does not ask you to write; it keeps the answers you get from an AI model as AI notes and decides which chapter each one goes into.

The price gap is tiny (1.99 versus 1 USD a month), so price will not decide this. The deciding question is who produces most of your text, you or a model. UpNote facts on this page were read on getupnote.com on 23 October 2026.

Every entry here started as a chat answer; the titles and summaries were written by the model.

## Revising for a biochemistry exam

Picture a second-year student with an UpNote notebook called "Biochem", sub-notebooks for each lecture and hashtags like #exam and #glycolysis. Lecture notes go in by hand, often during the lecture itself, on an iPad. That is exactly what UpNote is good at.

Then come the evenings. The student asks Claude why the Krebs cycle needs oxygen when no step uses it directly, asks for a comparison of NADH and FADH2, asks for five likely exam questions. Thirty answers a week, most of them useful, all of them stuck in a chat list.

In the chat-to-notes app the same evenings leave behind a vault "Biochem" with chapters such as `2.` Cellular respiration and `2.1` Krebs cycle. Each kept answer is an AI note with a short summary on top and the full exchange underneath. When the student asks about the electron transport chain on Thursday, the model receives the table of contents and the five most relevant AI notes (24,000 characters at most) and the screen lists which ones it used.

Lecture notes by hand, chat answers by the model. Two apps, one semester.

## Reasons to choose the AI notes route

- Most of your new material comes out of a chat with Claude, GPT, Gemini or a local Ollama model.
- You never get round to filing. Here the model picks a chapter by number, or asks for a new subchapter.
- You repeat questions without noticing. The duplicate check offers a merge, and the merge only appends.
- You hand in summaries. The vault exports to a PDF of summaries only, to full notes, or to a PowerPoint deck with 15 themes.
- You want notes as plain files on your own disk, with no account server holding them.

For people who also look at Obsidian alternatives, one practical detail: the exported files open in Obsidian unchanged. The [Obsidian alternative page](https://sumizai.com/alternative-to/sumizai-alternative-to-obsidian.html) covers that pairing in depth, and the [AI notes app](https://sumizai.com/) home page shows the whole flow.

## Reasons UpNote is the better notebook

You write a lot yourself, on several devices, and you want it everywhere. UpNote syncs automatically and works offline on phones, tablets and Linux. Nothing comparable exists on the other side: no mobile app yet, no sync at all.

You dislike subscriptions. 39.99 USD once and UpNote Premium is yours; the AI notes app is monthly only, and you also pay your AI provider per use.

You want version history for text you typed. UpNote keeps it. A free tier of 50 notes lets you test all of this before paying.

## Taking notes across

UpNote to SumizAI: export notes as Markdown (single notes or several combined) and give the folder to the importer. Files with `.md`, `.markdown`, `.txt` or `.pdf` go in one by one, and each becomes a new note whose source is your original text. Notebook names are not carried over as chapters; the model builds its own table of contents. Exporting each note separately gives better chapters than one combined file.

The reverse direction is easy to read but manual to import: the vault ZIP holds `base.md` and a `notes/` folder of Markdown files, and getupnote.com does not describe a Markdown import, so plan to copy notes over by hand.

## UpNote questions

### How much is UpNote?

Free for up to 50 notes. Premium is 1.99 USD a month or 39.99 USD for a lifetime licence; prices vary by region and tax.

### Does UpNote use AI?

Its home page lists no AI features. You type the notes.

### Which is better for students?

UpNote for lecture notes on an iPad, the AI notes app for the questions you ask a model afterwards. Many students would want both.

### Is SumizAI cheaper than UpNote?

Per month, yes (1 versus 1.99 USD), but you add the model costs from your own provider. Over several years UpNote's lifetime licence is cheaper.
