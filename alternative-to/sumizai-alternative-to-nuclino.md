# SumizAI vs Nuclino: a wiki for the team, a notebook for you

Nuclino calls itself “your team's collective brain”: a quick, uncluttered wiki where colleagues keep docs, project boards and knowledge in one shared space. SumizAI is a one-person tool. It turns your own conversations with an AI model into AI notes, filed into a table of contents on your Mac.

So the question is rarely “which is better”. It is whose knowledge you are trying to keep: a team's, written by people, or yours, written by a model from your chats. Nuclino details below were read on nuclino.com on 23 October 2026.

Chapters numbered `1.`, `1.1`, `a)`: the model picks the chapter for every new note.

## When Nuclino is the right call

Pick Nuclino whenever more than one person has to read and edit the same page. A product team with a spec, a support team with a how-to collection, an agency with client handbooks: that is its home ground, and a single-user notes app cannot stand in for it.

It also wins on reach. Nuclino runs in the browser and has apps for Windows, macOS, Linux, iOS and Android, so a colleague on a Chromebook or a phone joins in a minute. The Mac app here is not installable yet.

Two smaller points. The free plan is usable for a small trial (50 items), and the Business plan adds SAML single sign-on and an audit log, the kind of thing an IT department will ask about before anything else.

## One question, two places to keep the answer

Take a Tuesday afternoon. You ask Claude how to set up a retry policy for a flaky payment webhook, and the answer is good: backoff intervals, an idempotency key, a dead-letter queue.

In Nuclino you would create an item, paste the useful bits, rewrite them so a colleague can follow, and drop it into the “Backend” collection. Worth it, if the team needs that page. Most of the time you just needed the answer for yourself, and the item never gets written.

In the AI notes app the answer is filed as you read it. The model writes a title and a one-line summary, keeps your question and its reply verbatim as the source, and places the note under `4.2 Webhooks` in a vault called “Work”. Three weeks on, a question about refunds goes out with the table of contents and up to five of the closest AI notes (24,000 characters at most), and the webhook note is one of them. The app tells you so.

## Reasons to keep personal AI notes outside the team wiki

- Half-formed answers. A team wiki wants polished pages; your vault can hold the rough answer and its source.
- Duplicates sorted for you. Before saving, a second model rates similarity from 0 to 1 and asks whether to merge. Merging appends, nothing is overwritten.
- Your model, your bill. Anthropic, OpenAI, Google Gemini, Groq, OpenRouter, local Ollama or your own OpenAI-compatible server, paid directly on your key.
- Files you own. Every note is plain Markdown on your disk, and no server of ours receives notes, questions or documents.
- Cost. One person on Nuclino Business pays 10 USD a month for Sidekick; the [AI notes app](https://sumizai.com/) costs 1 USD plus model usage.

If you also looked at Obsidian for this job, the same reasoning applies there: SumizAI is an [Obsidian alternative for answers you got from AI](https://sumizai.com/alternative-to/sumizai-alternative-to-obsidian.html), and its exported vault opens in Obsidian as a normal folder. Among Obsidian alternatives it is the narrow one: no plugins, no graph, no canvas.

## Leaving Nuclino with your Markdown

Nuclino's help centre describes two exports. Any member can export a single item as Markdown, Word (`.docx`) or PDF; on the free plan, PDFs carry an “Exported from www.nuclino.com” footnote. A whole workspace goes out as Markdown in a ZIP, with images and files in a separate folder, via the ⋮ menu, Workspace settings, then Export Workspace. Owners and admins can switch exporting off, so ask first.

Unpack the ZIP and give the folder to the importer. It reads `.md`, `.markdown`, `.txt` and `.pdf`, one file after another, keeps each original as the note's source and rebuilds the table of contents after every file. Word files are not on the list.

Going the other way, the vault exports as a ZIP with `base.md` and a `notes/` folder of Markdown files with relative links.

## Frequently asked

### Can I share AI notes with my team?

Not inside the app: there is no sync and no collaboration. You can export a chapter to PDF or PowerPoint and send that, or keep shared knowledge in Nuclino.

### Does Nuclino Sidekick write notes from my chats?

Sidekick works inside Nuclino: it answers questions from your team's content, drafts text and pulls action items out of messy notes. It does not collect conversations you have with other AI models.

### Which AI model does Sidekick use?

The Sidekick page does not say. For your own AI notes you choose the provider and model yourself.

### Is Nuclino free for one person?

Yes, up to 50 items, 3 canvases and 2 GB of storage. Beyond that Starter starts at 6 USD a month on yearly billing.

### Will the app run in a browser like Nuclino?

No. It is a desktop app; the Mac version comes first and there is no web app to download.
