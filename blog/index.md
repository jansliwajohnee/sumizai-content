# SumizAI Blog

- [Why is SumizAI different from every other note editor?](https://sumizai.com/blog/why-sumizai-is-different-from-other-note-editors.html): A note editor hands you a blank page. SumizAI writes AI notes from your chats, files them in a chapter, checks for duplicates and saves plain .md files.
- [How do you actually work with SumizAI day to day?](https://sumizai.com/blog/how-to-work-with-sumizai.html): Connect a provider key, create a vault, ask questions and let AI notes file themselves. The daily rhythm, from the first question to an exported PDF or deck.
- [What is a vault, and why is it a boundary?](https://sumizai.com/blog/what-is-a-vault-in-sumizai.html): A vault holds your AI notes, their table of contents and duplicate settings, and it marks the edge of what the model sees when you ask a question.
- [How does a chat answer become a note?](https://sumizai.com/blog/how-a-chat-answer-becomes-a-note.html): One question followed from chat to file: how AI notes get a title, summary and chapter, how duplicates are judged, and what lands in notes/.
- [How does the table of contents file your notes?](https://sumizai.com/blog/how-the-table-of-contents-works.html): A chapter tree up to six levels deep, a model that files AI notes by picking a number, and a rebuild that lays the whole vault out again in batches of 60.
- [How does SumizAI stop your library filling with near-duplicates?](https://sumizai.com/blog/how-sumizai-handles-duplicate-notes.html): Up to 5 candidates from full-text and trigram search, a helper model scoring each from 0 to 1, three vault modes, and merges that keep AI notes append-only.
- [How do notes link to each other, and what links back?](https://sumizai.com/blog/links-between-notes-and-backlinks.html): How SumizAI links AI notes: plain Markdown links inside sentences, only to files that exist, a list of backlinks under each note, and renames that fix every link.
- [What does SumizAI search when you search?](https://sumizai.com/blog/how-search-works-in-sumizai.html): How search in your AI notes works: title, summary, body and the original chat, ranked by weight, typo-tolerant, plus a chat mode that finds a note by meaning.
- [Where does SumizAI store your notes?](https://sumizai.com/blog/where-sumizai-stores-your-notes.html): Your AI notes are plain .md files in a folder you choose: one directory per vault, base.md for the contents, notes/ for the notes. The database is only an index.
- [Which AI models does SumizAI work with?](https://sumizai.com/blog/which-ai-models-sumizai-works-with.html): AI notes written by Claude, GPT, Gemini, Groq, OpenRouter, a local Ollama or your own server, always on your own API key and with the model list read live.
- [How do you turn a vault into a PDF or a PowerPoint deck?](https://sumizai.com/blog/export-notes-to-pdf-and-powerpoint.html): Turn a vault of AI notes into a ZIP of Markdown files, a PDF with summaries or full notes, or an editable .pptx deck with 15 templates.
- [How does SumizAI compare with Notion, Obsidian and a chat window?](https://sumizai.com/blog/sumizai-vs-notion-obsidian-and-chatgpt.html): Where AI notes from SumizAI fit next to Notion, Obsidian and a plain ChatGPT or Claude window, where they do not compete, and when to use two tools together.
- [How do students and researchers use SumizAI?](https://sumizai.com/blog/sumizai-for-students-and-researchers.html): AI notes for a whole semester: one vault per subject, questions that make good notes, imported lecture PDFs and a revision PDF built from the contents.
- [What does SumizAI do for developers?](https://sumizai.com/blog/sumizai-for-developers.html): AI notes for engineering work: design decisions and debugging sessions saved as plain Markdown, with the original exchange kept verbatim under each note.
- [What does one dollar a month actually buy?](https://sumizai.com/blog/what-one-dollar-a-month-buys.html): What 1 USD a month pays for in an AI notes app: one plan after a 7-day trial, your own AI key billed by the provider, and notes that stay yours if you stop.
- [What is Markdown, and why does it matter?](https://sumizai.com/blog/markdown.html): Markdown explained with real examples: core syntax, CommonMark and GFM, how a .md file renders, and what AI notes look like when saved as Markdown.
- [How to turn Claude chats into Markdown notes](https://sumizai.com/blog/how-to-turn-claude-chats-into-markdown-notes.html): Claude models on your own Anthropic key, each useful answer saved as one of your AI notes in a plain .md file, plus what to do with chats already on claude.ai.
- [No more handwritten notes: what changes when AI notes write themselves](https://sumizai.com/blog/no-more-handwritten-notes.html): Manual note-taking breaks down in your busiest weeks. What replaces it: AI notes written from chat answers, filed, checked for duplicates, saved as Markdown.
- [How to save ChatGPT answers as notes](https://sumizai.com/blog/how-to-save-chatgpt-answers-as-notes.html): Two honest routes for turning ChatGPT answers into AI notes: GPT models inside SumizAI on your own OpenAI key, or pasting and importing text you already have.
