## What the app does

SumizAI is a note-taking application for macOS. You talk to an AI model of your choice, and the answers you want to keep become notes: the app writes the note, gives it a title, places it in the vault's table of contents, links it to related notes and checks whether it duplicates one you already have. You can ask questions about your own notes and import documents. Notes are plain Markdown files in a folder on your Mac.

The app requires macOS 12 or later and an internet connection to sign in, to verify your subscription and to reach a cloud AI provider.
