# Refund Policy

The short version: the SumizAI subscription is sold and charged by **Apple** through the App Store. Apple holds the payment, so a refund is requested from Apple at [reportaproblem.apple.com](https://reportaproblem.apple.com). We cannot send money back for an App Store purchase ourselves — but we are glad to help if something went wrong: [contact@sumizai.com](mailto:contact@sumizai.com).

## What you pay, and to whom

| What | Who charges it | Refunds |
|---|---|---|
| SumizAI subscription (monthly, auto-renewing, with a 7-day free trial for eligible new subscribers) | Apple, to your Apple ID | Requested from Apple — see below. |
| Usage of your AI provider (Anthropic, OpenAI, Google Gemini, Groq, OpenRouter, your own server) | That provider, under your own account and key | Not by us: that money never reaches us. Ask the provider. |
| A local Ollama | Nobody — it runs on your computer | Nothing to refund. |
| The website `sumizai.com` | Free | Nothing to refund. |

The terms of the subscription are in the [Terms of Service](https://sumizai.com/legal/terms.html).

## The free trial

New subscribers get 7 days free; Apple decides who is eligible (normally once per Apple ID). Nothing is charged during the trial. If you do not want to pay, cancel at least 24 hours before the trial ends in your Apple ID settings ([how to cancel](https://support.apple.com/en-us/118428)); otherwise the first month is charged when the trial ends.

## How to ask for a refund

- Go to [reportaproblem.apple.com](https://reportaproblem.apple.com) and sign in with the Apple ID you used to subscribe.
- Choose _Request a refund_, pick the reason and the SumizAI purchase.
- Apple decides and tells you the result. Apple's guide: [Request a refund for apps or content that you bought from Apple](https://support.apple.com/en-us/118223).

When Apple refunds a subscription, it tells our server, and access paid for by that purchase ends.

## Renewals and cancelling

The subscription renews every month until you turn off auto-renew, at least 24 hours before the end of the current period. After cancelling you keep access until the end of the period already paid for; Apple does not normally refund the remainder of a started month. Deleting the app or your SumizAI account does **not** cancel the subscription — only Apple can, in your Apple ID settings.

## Your statutory rights

Nothing on this page reduces the rights that Polish and EU law give consumers (and sole traders buying on consumer terms), including the rights relating to conformity of digital services with the contract. Apple, as the seller in the App Store, handles payment-related claims under its own terms; if something about the app itself does not work as promised, write to us at [contact@sumizai.com](mailto:contact@sumizai.com) and we will put it right or help you with your request to Apple.
